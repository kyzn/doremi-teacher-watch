# App Name

Doremi Teacher Watch Special

# Subtitle

Ear training on your wrist

# Promotional Text

Hear a note, name it. Names can follow standard pitch or your own instrument's tuning.

# Description

Train your ear on your Apple Watch.

A note plays, you pick its name from four. Replay as often as you like before answering. Every round ends with the right answer and a button to hear it again. No timers.

Choose Do Re Mi or A B C. Practice the seven natural notes, or add sharps and flats.

Play an instrument that names notes its own way? Tell the app which note your Re sounds like, and every name follows. Made for saz players, useful for any transposing instrument.

The Digital Crown adjusts the volume while you practice. Stats keep today's and all-time accuracy, for all rounds and per note set.

Runs on the watch alone. No iPhone app, no account, no ads, no tracking, no data collected.
