This is a standalone watchOS app. There is no iPhone app, no account, no
microphone use, no network access, and no third-party code.

DEMO VIDEO: attached in the Resolution Center (recorded from a physical Apple
Watch Series 10 through Apple Watch Mirroring on an iPhone, since watchOS has
no screen recording of its own). It shows launch, a round, Settings with the My instrument mapping,
and Stats. That is the entire app.

PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE: Ear training for music learners: a note plays, the
user names it. Made with saz players in mind, where the string called Re
commonly sounds E; the My instrument setting lets the app use the learner's
own note names.

SETUP AND ACCESS: None. Launch the app and tap Start.
- Start: a note plays automatically. Tap one of the four names to answer.
  Replay is unlimited before answering. Next plays a new note.
- Settings: Note names (Do Re Mi or A B C); Names follow (Standard, or
  My instrument, e.g. "My Re sounds like E", which shifts every name);
  Notes to practice (seven notes, or with sharps/flats).
- Stats: swipe between Total, Seven notes, and With sharps/flats.
- The Digital Crown adjusts volume on the practice screens.
No logins, credentials, or sample files.

EXTERNAL SERVICES: None. No network access. Tones are synthesized on the
watch. No data providers, authentication, payments, analytics, or AI.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES: None. Identical in every region. UI localized into
English, Japanese, and Turkish; Japanese shows solfège in katakana.

REGULATED CONTENT: Not applicable. No protected third-party material.

TESTED ON: Apple Watch Series 10 (watchOS 26) on the wrist, plus SE 3 40mm
and Series 11 46mm simulators. Sound plays through the watch speaker or
Bluetooth headphones; on the simulator through the Mac.

PRIVACY: No data is collected or transmitted. Settings and the correct/wrong
counts are stored only on the watch.
