This is a standalone watchOS app. There is no iPhone app, no account, no
microphone use, and no network access of any kind.

How to use it:
- Start: a note plays automatically. Tap one of the four names to answer.
  Replay is unlimited before answering. Next plays a new note.
- Settings: choose Do Re Mi or A B C names; choose Standard pitch or
  "My instrument" (e.g. a saz whose Re sounds like E, which shifts every name);
  choose seven notes or notes with sharps/flats.
- Stats: swipe between Total, Seven notes, and With sharps/flats.
- The Digital Crown adjusts volume on the practice screens.

Sound plays through the watch speaker or connected Bluetooth headphones. On the
watchOS Simulator sound plays through the Mac.

Privacy: no data is collected or transmitted. Settings and correct/wrong counts
are stored only on the watch.
